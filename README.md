# IsoRim Waylith - Holsters Pack

[Holsters](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2610120706) in the 3D view of [IsoRim Waylith](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRim-1.7) (RimWorld 1.7).

- Sheathed weapons sit on the 3D figures, as their 3D model or their art made solid
- Placed by your own Holsters settings, changes show at once
- Sideways, height and tilt come from the South settings, front to back and lean from the East settings; North and West do not change the 3D place

Requires IsoRim Waylith and Holsters. Load after them.

让 [Holsters](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2610120706) 的收纳武器显示在 [IsoRim Waylith](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRim-1.7) 的 3D 人物身上（RimWorld 1.7）。

- 收纳的武器挂在 3D 人物身上，显示为 3D 模型或立体化的贴图
- 位置来自你自己的 Holsters 设置，改动立即生效
- 左右、高度和倾斜取南向设置，前后和前倾取东向设置；北向和西向不影响 3D 位置

需要 IsoRim Waylith 和 Holsters。请放在它们之后加载。

Holsters is made by Iwo Rosiak; this pack contains no files of the original mod.
